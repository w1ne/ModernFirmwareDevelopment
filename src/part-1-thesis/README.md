# Part 1: The Thesis (Zero to One)

> "The challenge of the future is that we don't know what it looks like... but we know it will be different." — Peter Thiel

In this section, we argue that **firmware is no longer a support function for hardware**. It is the primary driver of value, differentiation, and safety in modern devices.

To build "Zero to One" products, we must abandon the "Flash and Pray" methods of the past and adopt a rigorous, software-defined approach inspired by the DORA metrics.
