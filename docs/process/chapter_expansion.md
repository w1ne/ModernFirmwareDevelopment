# Chapter Expansion Workflow

This document defines the standard operating procedure (SOP) for expanding a chapter from a "stub" to a "deep dive."

## 1. Research Phase
Before writing, conduct targeted research to find:
*   **Case Studies:** Real-world companies (e.g., Tesla, Dyson, Sonos) that exemplify the chapter's principle.
*   **Academic/Industry Links:** References to *Accelerate*, *Zero to One*, or major conference talks (Embedded World, RustConf).
*   **Counter-Examples:** "How it goes wrong" stories (e.g., Toyota unintended acceleration, bricked IoT devices).

## 2. Structure Template
Each chapter must follow this structure:
1.  **The Hook:** A provocative statement or story (The "Zero to One" concept).
2.  **The Theory:** The technical/process explanation (e.g., DORA metrics definition).
3.  **Real-World Example:** A concrete case study.
    *   *Format:* "Case Study: [Company Name] - [Success/Failure]"
4.  **The Implementation:** How to do it today (Code snippets, config examples).
5.  **References/Further Reading:** External links.

## 3. Writing Guidelines
*   **Voice:** Authoritative, pragmatic, slightly contrarian (anti-status-quo).
*   **Evidence:** "Don't just say testing is good. Show that it reduces lead time by 90%."
*   **Links:** Link generously to internal chapters and external tools.

## 4. Verification
*   Does the chapter have at least one real-world company reference?
*   Is there a code snippet or diagram description?
*   Does it link to at least 2 external sources?
